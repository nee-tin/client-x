Platform Guide
Everything You Need to Know
Zinkly makes it simple to find, book, and learn from the world's greatest music artists.
01
Create Your Free Account
Sign up in under 60 seconds. Tell us your musical goals, current skill level, and what you want to learn. Zinkly personalizes your experience from day one.
02
Browse & Discover Artists
Filter 500+ verified artists by genre, discipline, price, rating, and availability. Read bios, watch intro videos, and listen to their music on Apple Music.
03
Choose Your Session Format
Select from 1-on-1 private lessons, small group masterclasses, or comprehensive monthly mentorship packages — whatever fits your budget and goals.
04
Book & Pay Securely
Pick your time slot and pay securely via Razorpay. Receive instant confirmation, calendar invite, and your video session link.

Attend Your Live Session
Join your HD video session at the scheduled time. All sessions are recorded so you can review your lesson at any time within 48 hours.
06
Grow, Track & Repeat
Track your progress, receive post-session notes, and follow up with your artist. Build a long-term relationship and watch your skills soar.
